# Centrally-Excited-Random-Walk

A machine-checked **Lean 4** formalization of the paper

> Ahmed Bou-Rabee and Yuval Peres,
> *Limit shapes of centrally excited random walks*,
> pinned at [`paper/limit-shapes.tex`](paper/limit-shapes.tex).

The whole revised paper is formalized: Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2, Theorem 1.3 (in four parts)
and Theorem 2.1, and every lemma and proposition of Sections 2 to 9.

The development is built on [mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) and on
the shared library
[Lattice-Probability](https://github.com/nitromannitol/Lattice-Probability) (`LatticeProb`),
which supplies lattice sites, the simple random walk and its Green function, the asymptotics of
the lattice potential kernel and Green function, Freedman's inequality, the Paley–Zygmund
inequality and the Cramér–Wold device.

## What is proved

- **Theorem 1.1, the limit shape** (`CERW.Frozen.limit_shape`): for `d ≥ 2` and `0 < ε < 1/d`,
  almost surely the departure range is the Euclidean ball of radius
  `r_n = ((d+1)n/(2d ε ω_d))^{1/(d+1)}`, the local times converge to the cone
  `2 d ε (r_n − |x|)_+`, and every site is visited infinitely often.
- **Theorem 1.2, the fluctuation bounds** (`CERW.Frozen.fluctuation_rates`): quantitative
  bounds on the inner and outer radii, the volume of the occupied cells and the local times,
  with rates `√(r_n log n)` in the plane and `log n` in `d ≥ 3`.
- **Theorem 1.3, sharpness** (`CERW.Frozen.sharp_*`): the planar rates cannot be improved, with
  matching lower bounds on the radii and the local times.
- **Theorem 2.1, the limit shape for a norm** (`CERW.Frozen.norm_shape`) and the potential of a
  norm ball (Lemma 2.2).
- **Sections 2 to 9**: every lemma and proposition the paper uses, including the local-time and
  potential estimates, the coarse bounds, the inner- and outer-radius arguments, the limit laws
  and the lower bounds on the fluctuations.

The only assumed results are the two martingale limit theorems the paper cites for its limit
laws: Hall and Heyde's martingale central limit theorem and Stout's martingale law of the
iterated logarithm. Of Stout's law only the lower half (Stout 1970, Theorem 2) is assumed: the
upper half (Theorem 1) is proved, as `CERW.Generic.Martingale.Lil.stout_upper`. Each is a proposition in [`CERW/External/`](CERW/External/) and an explicit
hypothesis of every theorem whose proof uses it.

## Guarantees

- **No `sorry`** in the library. The comparator challenge under `CERWAudit/` contains one
  intentional statement-level `sorry`, which its solution file proves.
- **No custom axiom.** Each registered declaration depends only on mathlib's standard axioms
  `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; `CERTIFICATE.md` records the axiom closure and
  the commands that reproduce it.
- **Independent check of the statement.** The limit shape theorem is restated, with the model
  rebuilt from Mathlib primitives alone, in `CERWAudit/LimitShape/Challenge.lean`; the workflow
  submits it and its solution to
  [leanprover/comparator](https://github.com/leanprover/comparator). See
  [`CERWAudit/README.md`](CERWAudit/README.md).
- **Pinned toolchain.** Lean `v4.32.0`, mathlib `81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997`,
  and `Lattice-Probability` at a fixed revision, recorded in
  [`lake-manifest.json`](lake-manifest.json).

## Building

The toolchain is pinned in [`lean-toolchain`](lean-toolchain) and managed by
[elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan).

```bash
lake exe cache get   # prebuilt mathlib
lake build           # the library
lake build CERWAudit # the Mathlib-only comparator surface
```

`import CERW` loads the whole development and `import CERW.Frozen` the frozen statements.

## Repository layout

```
CERW/
  Frozen/      the frozen statements, one declaration per file, with their paper labels
  Model/       the model: sites, the one-step kernel, the CERW law, local times, ranges
  Support/     the proofs, below the frozen statements in the import graph
  Generic/     results independent of the paper
  External/    the two cited results that are assumed
  Meta/        the axiom report
CERW.lean      the root module, importing the whole library
CERWAudit/     the Mathlib-only restatement and its solution
ledger/        the frozen-statement manifest, readings, audits and approvals
paper/         the pinned papers
```
