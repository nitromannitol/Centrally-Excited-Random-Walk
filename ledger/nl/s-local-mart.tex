% s-local-mart: eq:localmart.
\paragraph{Statement.} Under the one-step bound, for $p>0$ there is $C$ such that for $n\geq2$, with probability at least $1-Cn^{-p}$: $|\mathcal M^y_n|\leq C(\sqrt{B_yL}+L)$ for all lattice
$|y|\leq3n$, where $B_y=\sum_{j<n}(1+|X_j-y|)^{2-2d}$.
\paragraph{Proof.} As in s-interval-mart with $s=0$, $t=n$: clamp the increments, take the bracket $V_t=C_g^2\sum_{j<t}(1+|X_j-y|)^{2-2d}$, apply s-dyadic with $W=\max(C_g^2n,1)$
and $K=p+d+2$, and use $\sqrt{\max(a,1)L}\leq\sqrt{aL}+L$. The union is over at most $(6n+1)^d$ targets.
