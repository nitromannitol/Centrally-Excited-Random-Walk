% s-radial-packing: the radial packing inequality. Sub-step of s-mass (eq:massidentity).
\paragraph{Statement.} For bounded measurable $D\subset\R^d$, $d\geq1$:
$\int_D|v|\dd v\geq\frac d{d+1}\omega_d^{-1/d}|D|^{1+1/d}$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $\int_{B(0,\rho)}|v|\dd v=\sigma_d\rho^{d+1}/(d+1)$ by polar coordinates. By the bathtub
principle, applied to the radially increasing weight $|v|$, the integral over $D$ is at least that
over the ball of the same volume, $\rho=(|D|/\omega_d)^{1/d}$. Substituting gives the display.
