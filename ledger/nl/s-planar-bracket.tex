% s-planar-bracket: the bound on B_z in the planar contact variance.
\paragraph{Statement.} There is $C$ with: if $|x-z|\leq R$ ($R\geq1$) and $\ell_n(x)\leq c_0(b-|x|)_++A$ for every $x\in A_n$, with $|z|\geq b$, then
$\sum_{x\in A_n}\ell_n(x)(1+|x-z|)^{-2}\leq C(c_0(R+1)+A\log(R+2))$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Bound termwise. Reindex $x=z+w$ with $|w|\leq R$. Then $\sum(b-|z+w|)_+(1+|w|)^{-2}\leq C(R+1)$ (s-planar-contact-sum), and
$\sum_{x\in A_n}(1+|x-z|)^{-2}\leq C\log(R+2)$ (s-centered-sums with $d=2$).
