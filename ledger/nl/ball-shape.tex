\paragraph{Paper (verbatim).} ``Then the following statements hold almost surely. (i) For every
$0<\eta<a$, for all sufficiently large integers $n$,
$B(0,(a-\eta)N)\cap\Z^d\subseteq A_n\subseteq V_n\subseteq B(0,(a+\eta)N)\cap\Z^d$. (ii) The departure
local times satisfy $\sup_{x\in\Z^d}|\frac{\ell_n(x)}N-2d\eps(a-\frac{|x|}N)_+|\to0$. (iii) The range
satisfies $|V_n|/N^d\to\omega_da^d$. For every fixed $x\in\Z^d$, the local time satisfies
$\ell_n(x)/N\to2d\eps a$; in particular, every vertex is visited infinitely often.''
\paragraph{Proof.} By the second conjunct of \texttt{thm-fluctuations} there is a deterministic
$C$, and a.s. an $n_1(\omega)$, with $\mathrm{Good}(C,X(\omega),n)$ for $n\ge n_1$. Since
$Q_n\to0$ and $Q_n^{1/d}L_n\to0$, for $0<\eta<a$ eventually $CQ_n\le\eta$ and $CQ_n^{1/d}L_n\le\eta$.
Monotonicity of the balls in the radius gives (i). (ii) follows from the profile clause, since
$CQ_n^{1/d}\to0$. For (iii), $|V_n|\le|A_n|+1$ (\texttt{visitedRange\_eq\_insert}) and
$|A_n|\le|V_n|$, while $|A_n|/N^d\to\omega_da^d$ by the volume clause and $1/N^d\to0$. For fixed $x$,
$|x|/N\to0$ and the profile clause give $\ell_n(x)/N\to2d\eps a>0$. So $\ell_n(x)\to\infty$, and
$X_j=x$ for infinitely many $j$. The single null set does not depend on $\eta$ or $x$.
\paragraph{Transcription notes.} One $\forall^\mathrm{a.e.}$ precedes ``for every $\eta$'' and ``for
every $x$'', as the paper's ``the following statements hold almost surely'' and its proof
(``on the same probability-one event'') require. (ii) is written in the eventual form (R-5).
``Visited infinitely often'' is $\exists^\mathrm{f}j,\ X_j=x$.
