% s-potential-integrable: integrability of the Newtonian field. Sub-step of eq:potential-bound, eq:packing, eq:newton.
\paragraph{Statement.} For $d\geq1$ and $\rho>0$: $|v-y|^{1-d}$ is integrable on $B(y,\rho)$ with
$\int_{B(y,\rho)}|v-y|^{1-d}\dd v=\sigma_d\rho$. For measurable $D$ of finite volume it is integrable
on $D$ and $\int_D|v-y|^{1-d}\dd v\leq\sigma_d(|D|/\omega_d)^{1/d}$, uniformly in $y$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Polar coordinates: the radial integrand $t^{d-1}t^{1-d}=1$ on $(0,\rho)$ gives
$\sigma_d\rho$. Translate by $y$. For $D$, the bathtub principle: among sets of volume $|D|$ the
integral of the radially decreasing kernel is largest on the ball $B(y,\rho)$ with
$\omega_d\rho^d=|D|$.
