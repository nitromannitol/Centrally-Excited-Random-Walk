% s-card-tail: eq:coarse-cellcount (general form).
\paragraph{Statement.} If $S\subseteq A_n$, $\sqrt d/2<\rho\leq R'$, and $\rho<|x|<R'$ on $S$, then $|S|\leq\sigma_d(R'+\sqrt d/2)^{d-1}F(\rho-\sqrt d/2)$.
\paragraph{Proof.} The cells of $S$ are disjoint unit cubes in $D_n\cap\{\rho-\sqrt d/2<|v|<R'+\sqrt d/2\}$ (s-cell-norm), where
$|v|^{1-d}\geq(R'+\sqrt d/2)^{1-d}$. So $\sigma_dF(\rho-\sqrt d/2)\geq(R'+\sqrt d/2)^{1-d}|S|$.
