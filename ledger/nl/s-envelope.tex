% s-envelope: the local-time envelope and the inner inclusion (inputs of eq:resolvent, eq:envelopeplanar, eq:sandwich).
\paragraph{Statement.} If $B(0,b)\subseteq D_n$, $b>0$, and $\ell_n(y)=U_{D_n}(y)+\rho-M$, then
$\ell_n(y)\leq2d\eps(b-|y|)_++2d\eps\omega_d^{-1/d}|D_n\setminus B(0,b)|^{1/d}+|\rho|+|M|$. If $B(0,b)\subseteq D_n$ and $|x|<b$ then $x\in A_n$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $U_{D_n}=U_{B(0,b)}+U_E$ (disjoint union, both integrable), $U_{B(0,b)}(y)=2d\eps(b-|y|)_+$ (s-newton), and $|U_E|\leq2d\eps\omega_d^{-1/d}|E|^{1/d}$ (s-potential-bound).
For the inclusion: $x\in C_x$ as a point of $\R^d$ with $|x|<b$, so $x\in D_n$ and hence $x=\mathrm{cellCenter}(x)\in A_n$.
