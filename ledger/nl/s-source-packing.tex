% s-source-packing: the fresh source sum is O(k^(1/d)) (eq:packing-lattice applied in the proof of eq:interval).
\paragraph{Statement.} Under the one-step bound: $|\sum_{x\in F}u_x\cdot Db(x-y)|\leq C_d|F|^{1/d}$ for every finite $F$ and every $y$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Each coordinate of $Db(z)$ is at most $C_g(1+|z|)^{1-d}$ in absolute value (the one-step bound at $z$ with $\pm e_i$), so
$|Db(z)|\leq\sqrt dC_g(1+|z|)^{1-d}$, and $|u_x|\leq1$. Apply s-packing-lattice.
