\paragraph{Paper (verbatim).} ``On the event in Theorem 1.2, the Euclidean Hausdorff distance satisfies
$d_H(N^{-1}V_n,\overline B(0,a))\leq C\,n^{-1/12}L^{5/4}$ ($d=2$), $C\,n^{-1/((d+1)(2d-1))}L^{2d/(2d-1)}$
($d\geq3$). The inner-radius error in the plane is at most $Cn^{1/6}\sqrt{\log(n+2)}$ in lattice
units.''
\paragraph{Proof.} Take the first conjunct of \texttt{thm-fluctuations} with constant $C$. On its
event, every point of $N^{-1}V_n$ has norm $<a+CQ^{1/d}L$, so it lies within $CQ^{1/d}L$ of
$\overline B(0,a)$. Every point $w$ of $\overline B(0,a)$ lies within $2CQ+2\sqrt d/N$ of
$N^{-1}V_n$. Put $w'=(1-(CQ+\sqrt d/N)/a)\,w$, so $|w'|\le a-CQ-\sqrt d/N$ (for large $n$ the
factor is in $(0,1)$). The lattice site $x$ nearest $Nw'$ has $|x-Nw'|\le\sqrt d/2$, so
$|x|<(a-CQ)N$ and $x\in A_n\subseteq V_n$ by the inner inclusion; and
$|w-x/N|\le|w-w'|+|w'-x/N|\le CQ+\sqrt d/N+\sqrt d/(2N)$. Since $Q\le Q^{1/d}L$ for large $n$ and $1/N=o(Q^{1/d}L)$, we get
$d_H\le C'Q^{1/d}L$. For $d=2$, $Q^{1/2}L=(L/N)^{1/4}L=n^{-1/12}L^{5/4}$. For $d\ge3$,
$Q^{1/d}L=(L/N)^{1/(2d-1)}L=n^{-1/((d+1)(2d-1))}L^{2d/(2d-1)}$. For the planar remark,
$CQN=C\sqrt{NL}=Cn^{1/6}\sqrt L$, and the inner inclusion is the claim. The probability bound is
the one of \texttt{thm-fluctuations}, with $C''=\max(C,C')$.
\paragraph{Transcription notes.} The distance is $\mathrm{hausdorffEDist}$ in
$\R_{\ge0}^\infty$ (R-5). ``On the event of Theorem 1.2'' is transcribed as a probability bound with
its own constant (R-7). The planar remark is the second conjunct, guarded by $d=2$.
