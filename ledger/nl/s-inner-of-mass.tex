\paragraph{Statement.} For $\varepsilon,C_0,C_1>0$, $C_m\geq0$ there are $C>0$, $n_0$: for $n\geq n_0$, on $\mathrm{fluctEvent}$ with $b$ the inradius, $m\leq C_mN^dQ$ implies

$\max(C_m,C_1)$: path clause, $|X_n|\leq H_n\leq C_0N$, $|A_n|\leq C_0N^d$, quadratic clause. \texttt{exists\_inner\_clauses} with $K'=a+C_0+3$ and $\delta_0=C_1e(M_n)$:
\texttt{error\_scale\_le} with $M_n\leq C_0N$ gives $\delta_0\leq C(\sqrt NL)$ ($d=2$) or $C(\sqrt{NL}+L)$ ($d\geq3$), eventually $\leq NQ^{1/d}$; the global clause covers $\|y\|<K'N\leq2n$.
