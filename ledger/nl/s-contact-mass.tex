% s-contact-mass: the contact inequality m <= C S^(d-1) (|rho| + |M| + Delta).
\paragraph{Statement.} If $B(0,b)\subseteq D_n\subseteq\bar B(0,S)$, $|y_0|=b$, $\ell_n(z)=0$, $\ell_n(z)=U(z)+\rho-M$ and $|U(y_0)-U(z)|\leq\Delta$, then
$|D_n\setminus B(0,b)|\leq\frac{\omega_d}{2\eps}2^{d-1}S^{d-1}(|\rho|+|M|+\Delta)$.
\paragraph{Proof.} By s-contact-lower, $\frac{2\eps}{\omega_d}2^{1-d}S^{1-d}m\leq U(y_0)\leq U(z)+\Delta=M-\rho+\Delta\leq|\rho|+|M|+\Delta$.
