\paragraph{Statement.} $U_{B(0,b)}(y)=2d\varepsilon(b-|y|)_+$ for every $b>0$ and $y$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Radial symmetry of $U_{B(0,b)}$ by coordinate reflections, then eq:newton: the spherical mean at radius $s=|y|$ is $2d\varepsilon F(s)$ and $F(s)=(b-s)_+$ for the ball.
