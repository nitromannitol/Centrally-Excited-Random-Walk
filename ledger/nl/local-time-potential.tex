\paragraph{Paper (verbatim).} ``For every $p>0$ there is $C=C(d,\eps,p)$ such that, for every integer
$n\geq2$, with probability at least $1-Cn^{-p}$, the following estimates hold simultaneously:
$M_n\leq C_d\eps R_n^{1/d}+C\lambda_n$; $M_{s,t}\leq C_d\eps k_{s,t}^{1/d}+C\lambda_n$ $(0\leq s<t\leq n)$;
$|\widetilde\ell_n(y)-U_{D_n}(y)|\leq C e_n(M_n)$ $(y\in\R^d,\ |y|\leq2n)$.''
Here $e_n(m)=\sqrt mL+L$ ($d=2$), $\sqrt{mL}+L$ ($d\ge3$); $\lambda_n=L^2$ ($d=2$), $L$ ($d\ge3$);
$L=\log(n+2)$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Fix $d\ge2$, $0<\eps<1/d$, $p>0$, a realization $X$ with \texttt{IsCERW}, and
$n\ge2$. Let $b$ be the kernel of \texttt{s-kernel-props}, with $(P-I)b=\ind_{\{0\}}$ and endpoint
growth $\sup_{|u|,|w|\le4n}|b(u)-b(w)|\le C_bL$ ($d=2$) or $\le C_b$ ($d\ge3$).
\emph{Step 1 (Dynkin).} For every lattice $y$, \texttt{s-dynkin-local} gives a martingale
$\mathcal M^y$ with
$\ell_t(y)=b(X_t-y)-b(-y)+\eps\sum_{j<t}I_ju_{X_j}\cdot Db(X_j-y)-\mathcal M^y_t$.
Its increments are bounded by $2C_\nabla$, and its conditional variances satisfy
$\langle\mathcal M^y\rangle_{s,t}\le C_d\sum_x\ell_{s,t}(x)(1+|x-y|)^{2-2d}$. The drift uses
\texttt{s-step-mean}, and the bounds use \texttt{s-gradient}.
\emph{Step 2 (event).} By \texttt{s-weight-sums} the weights over $|x-y|\le4n$ sum to $O(L)$ when
$d=2$ and to $O(1)$ when $d\ge3$. So the bracket over $[s,t]$ is at most $C M_{s,t}L$ or
$C M_{s,t}$. Apply \texttt{s-dyadic} to the $O(n^{d+2})$ martingales $\mathcal M^y_{s+\cdot}-\mathcal M^y_s$
with lattice $|y|\le3n$ and $0\le s<t\le n$, at dyadic bracket levels up to $n$. This gives an event
$G$ with $\mu(G^c)\le C n^{-p}$ on which $|\mathcal M^y_t-\mathcal M^y_s|\le C_1e_n(M_{s,t})$ for all
such $y,s,t$ (eq:interval-mart, \texttt{s-interval-mart}). The rest is deterministic on $G$.
\emph{Step 3 (interval bound).} Subtract Step 1 at $s$ and $t$ and take $y=X_j$ for $j\in[s,t)$ with
$\ell_{s,t}(y)=M_{s,t}$; this $y$ is admissible since $|y|\le n$. The endpoint difference is at most
$C_bL$ or $C_b$. The source term is at most
$\eps C_\nabla\sum_{x\in E}(1+|x-y|)^{1-d}\le\eps C_\nabla C_{\rm pack}k_{s,t}^{1/d}$, where $E$ is
the set of first-departure sites in $[s,t)$ and $|E|=k_{s,t}$ (\texttt{s-packing-lattice}).
Hence $M_{s,t}\le C_d\eps k_{s,t}^{1/d}+C_bL+C_1e_n(M_{s,t})$, and the Young absorption
(\texttt{Generic.Young.le\_two\_mul\_add\_sq\_of\_le\_add\_mul\_sqrt}) gives
$M_{s,t}\le 2C_d\eps k_{s,t}^{1/d}+C\lambda_n$ (\texttt{s-local-young}). The coefficient of
$\eps k^{1/d}$ depends only on $d$. The case $s=0,t=n$, with $k_{0,n}=R_n$
(\texttt{Occupation.freshCount\_zero}) and $M_{0,n}=M_n$, gives eq:M.
\emph{Step 4 (approximation).} At a lattice $y$ with $|y|\le3n$, Step 1 with $t=n$ gives
$\ell_n(y)=\eps\sum_{x\in A_n}u_x\cdot Db(x-y)+O(L)-\mathcal M^y_n$, and $|\mathcal M^y_n|\le C_1e_n(M_n)$.
\texttt{s-kernel-replace} compares $\eps\sum_{x\in A_n}u_x\cdot Db(x-y)$ with $U_{D_n}(y)$ up to $CL$
in three replacements: Db by the continuum kernel (error from \texttt{s-gradient} and
\texttt{s-weight-sums}); each kernel value by its cell integral (far cells $O((1+|x-y|)^{-d})$,
near cells by local integrability, \texttt{s-potential-integrable}); and the direction $u_x$ by
$u_v$ (\texttt{s-direction-error}). For real $y$ with $|y|\le2n$, let $x_y$ be its cell centre;
$|x_y|\le3n$, $\widetilde\ell_n(y)=\ell_n(x_y)$, and \texttt{s-cell-shift} gives
$|U_{D_n}(y)-U_{D_n}(x_y)|\le CL$. Since $e_n(m)\ge L$, all $O(L)$ errors are absorbed into
$C e_n(M_n)$.
\emph{Constants.} $C_d$ comes from \texttt{s-gradient} and \texttt{s-packing-lattice} only. $C$
comes from $d,\eps,p$ (Step 2 and the absorption). Neither depends on $n$ or on the realization.
\paragraph{Transcription notes.} The probability bound is on the outer measure of the bad event
(R-4). $C_d$ is bound before $\eps$ (R-15). $\sup_y$ is written $\forall y$. $M_n$, $M_{s,t}$ are
\texttt{Finset.sup} maxima; the empty case is $0$, which is correct for nonnegative local times.
