% s-radius-arith: absorbing the mass error (the last step of prop:coarse).
\paragraph{Statement.} For $c,c_0>0$, $C\geq0$ there is $C'$ such that for large $n$: if $s\geq c_0N$ and $cs^{d+1}\leq n+Cs^{d+1/2}L$ then $s\leq C'N$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $Cs^{d+1/2}L=Cs^{d+1}L/\sqrt s\leq Cs^{d+1}L/\sqrt{c_0N}\leq\frac c2s^{d+1}$ once $L/\sqrt N\leq c\sqrt{c_0}/(2C)$. Then $s^{d+1}\leq2n/c$, i.e.\
$s\leq(2/c)^{1/(d+1)}N$.
