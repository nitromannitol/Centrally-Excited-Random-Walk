\paragraph{Statement.} If $D_n\subseteq B(0,S)$ and $|\widetilde\ell_n-U_{D_n}|\leq\delta$ on $B(0,S)$, then $|n-\int_{B(0,S)}U_{D_n}|\leq\omega_dS^d\delta$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $\int_{B(0,S)}\widetilde\ell_n=n$ (s-cell-integral). $U_{D_n}$ is continuous (s-holder) and bounded (s-potential-bound), hence integrable on the ball.
$|\int(\widetilde\ell_n-U)|\leq\int|\widetilde\ell_n-U|\leq|B(0,S)|\delta=\omega_dS^d\delta$.
