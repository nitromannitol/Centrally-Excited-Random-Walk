% s-ball-excess: the first moment of a set containing a centred ball (used in eq:inradius).
\paragraph{Statement.} If $B(0,b)\subseteq D\subseteq\bar B(0,S)$, $D$ measurable, $b\geq0$: $\int_D|v|=\frac{d\omega_d}{d+1}b^{d+1}+\int_E|v|$ with $E=D\setminus B(0,b)$,
and $0\leq\int_E|v|\leq S|E|$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $D=B(0,b)\sqcup E$, and $\int_{B(0,b)}|v|=d\omega_db^{d+1}/(d+1)$ (s-radial-packing). On $E$, $0\leq|v|\leq S$, and $E$ has finite volume.
