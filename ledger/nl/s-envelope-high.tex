\paragraph{Statement.} For $d\geq3$ there is $C$: if $\ell_n(y)\leq c_0s_b(y)+A_1+A_2\sqrt{B_yL}$ at departure sites ($|X_j|\leq n$), then $\ell_n\leq C(c_0s_b+A_1+A_2^2L+c_0L)$ everywhere and $B_z\leq C(A_1+A_2^2L+c_0L)$ for $b\leq|z|\leq n$.
\paragraph{Proof.} (fl:912--957.) Young: $A_2\sqrt{B_yL}\leq\lambda B_y+A_2^2L/(4\lambda)$ with $\lambda k_0<1/2$; iterate the resolvent inequality and sum the geometric series using the kernel moments (\texttt{Generic/Lattice/SummableSums}); at $z$, $s_b(x)\leq|x-z|$.
