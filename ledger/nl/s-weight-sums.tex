% s-weight-sums: lattice weight sums (backfilled twin).
\paragraph{Statement.} $\sum_{|z|\leq R}(1+|z|)^{-d}\leq C\log(R+2)$; for $d\geq3$, $\sum_z(1+|z|)^{2-2d}<\infty$, and for $d\geq4$ its first moment is finite; the convolution of a nonnegative weight with a $1$-Lipschitz profile is bounded by mass times profile plus the first moment.
\paragraph{Proof.} Compare lattice shells with $\int_1^R r^{d-1}r^{-d}\,dr$ (shell counts $O(r^{d-1})$); the convolution bound is $s(x)\leq s(y)+|x-y|$ summed against the weight.
