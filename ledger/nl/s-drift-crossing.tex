% s-drift-crossing -- lem:crossing (limit-shapes.tex:662-667), deterministic form.
\paragraph{Statement.} Let $Z_t=X_t+\varepsilon\sum_{j<t}I_j\xi(X_j)$ with $I_j=1\{X_j\notin A_j\}$, $\varepsilon\ge0$.
If $|Z_t-Z_s|\le C\sqrt{(t-s)\log n}$ for all $0\le s<t\le n$, $|u|=1$, and $u\cdot\xi(X_j)\ge0$ at every
first departure $s\le j<t$, then $u\cdot(X_t-X_s)\le C\sqrt{(t-s)\log n}$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $u\cdot(X_t-X_s)=u\cdot(Z_t-Z_s)-\varepsilon\sum_{s\le j<t}I_j\,u\cdot\xi(X_j)
\le u\cdot(Z_t-Z_s)\le|u||Z_t-Z_s|$ (Cauchy--Schwarz, \texttt{real\_inner\_le\_norm}); the sum over
$[s,t)$ is the difference of the sums over $[0,t)$ and $[0,s)$ (\texttt{Finset.sum\_range\_add\_sum\_Ico}
or \texttt{Finset.range\_eq\_Ico}); each term is $\ge0$.
