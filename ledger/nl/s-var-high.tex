\paragraph{Statement.} For $d\geq3$ there is $C$: if $|X_j|\leq n$ for $j<n$ and at every departure site $\ell_n(y)\leq c_0s_b(y)+A_1+A_2\sqrt{B_yL}$, with
$B_y=\sum_x\ell_n(x)k(x-y)$ and $k(w)=(1+|w|)^{2-2d}$, then $\ell_n\leq C(c_0s_b+A_1+A_2^2L+c_0L)$ everywhere, and $B_z\leq C(A_1+A_2^2L+c_0L)$ for $b\leq|z|\leq n$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Let $S$ be the lattice ball of radius $2n$. For a departure site $y$, $B_y=\sum_{w\in S}k(w)\ell_n(y-w)$ ($k$ is even). Choose $\lambda=1/(2k_0)$ with
$k_0\geq\sum_Sk$ uniformly (s-weight-sums, summable). Young gives $A_2\sqrt{B_yL}\leq\lambda B_y+A_2^2L/(4\lambda)$. So
$\ell_n\leq c_0s_b+(A_1+A_2^2L/(4\lambda))+\lambda\sum_Sk(w)\ell_n(\cdot-w)$ everywhere (trivially off $A_n$). Since $s_b$ is $1$-Lipschitz,
$\sum_Sk(w)s_b(y-w)\leq k_0s_b(y)+J$ (\texttt{sum\_mul\_le\_of\_lipschitz}), with $J=\sum_S|w|k(w)\leq CL$ (d=3) or $C$ ($d\geq4$). Then
\texttt{le\_of\_le\_add\_conv} gives the envelope. For $b\leq|z|\leq n$: $s_b(x)\leq(|z|-|x|)_+\leq|x-z|$, and summing the envelope against $k(\cdot-z)$ over $A_n$
gives $B_z\leq C(J+k_0(A_1+A_2^2L+c_0L))$.
