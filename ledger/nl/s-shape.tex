\paragraph{Statement.} If $\mathrm{Good}_C(Y,n)$ holds for all large $n$, then $Y$ satisfies the five clauses of thm:shape: the ball inclusions, uniform profile
convergence, $|V_n|/N^d\to\omega_da^d$, $\ell_n(x)/N\to2d\eps a$ for each fixed $x$, and recurrence.
\paragraph{Proof.} By s-scale-limits, $Q\to0$, $Q^{1/d}L\to0$, and so $Q^{1/d}\to0$. For $\eta>0$, eventually $CQ\leq\eta$ and $CQ^{1/d}L\leq\eta$; insert these in the
inclusions and the profile bound. The volume clause gives $|\,|A_n|/N^d-\omega_da^d|\leq CQ\to0$. Also $V_n=A_n\cup\{X_n\}$ and $N^d\to\infty$. At fixed $x$,
$\ell_n(x)/N$ is within $CQ^{1/d}$ of $2d\eps(a-|x|/N)_+\to2d\eps a>0$. Hence $\ell_n(x)\to\infty$, which forces infinitely many visits.
