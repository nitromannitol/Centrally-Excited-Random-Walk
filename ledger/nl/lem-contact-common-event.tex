\begin{lemma}\label{lem:contact}
There exists $C(d,\drift,\gauge,p)<\infty$ such that, on the event of Section~\ref{sec:norm-event}, for large~$n$,
\begin{equation*}
H\leq Cr_nq_n=C\begin{cases}\sqrt{r_n\log n},&d=2,\\\log n,&d\geq3\end{cases}\, .
\end{equation*}
\end{lemma}
\end{verbatim}

\paragraph{Proof of the registered statement.}
Fix the source parameters and $p>0$. Apply the certified common-event theorem
\texttt{sec-common-event} to obtain seven positive constants, an exceptional
probability coefficient $C_p$, and a deterministic threshold $n_p$.
These constants precede the subgradient field and realization.
Apply the deterministic contact theorem \texttt{oct13-lem-contact}
to these constants to obtain $C_t>0$ and a threshold $n_t$.
Choose $C=C_p+C_t$ and $n_0=\max(n_p,n_t)$.

For any realization, the probability bound follows by monotonicity from
$C_p\leq C$. The cylinder law implies almost surely that the path starts
at zero and every increment is a unit step. On each such path in the
literal sixfold event, apply the deterministic contact estimate. Since
$r_n>0$ and $q_n\geq0$, increasing $C_t$ to $C$ preserves its bound.
This proves the probability and contact assertions together, with no
unproduced event constants or additional estimates.
