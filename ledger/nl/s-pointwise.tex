\paragraph{Statement.} Under the kernel facts, there is $C$ with $|\ell_n(y)-U_{D_n}(y)+\mathcal M^y_n|\leq CL$ for lattice $|y|\leq3n$, $0\leq\eps\leq1$, on paths from the origin with
$|X_j|\leq j$.

with $R'=4n+\sqrt d$ (then $|x-y|\leq4n$, $C_x\subseteq B(0,n+\sqrt d)$, $|y|\leq2R'$). Also $\log(4n+\sqrt d+2)\leq C_d\log(n+2)$.
