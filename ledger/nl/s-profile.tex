\paragraph{Statement.} Under $|\widetilde\ell_n-U_{D_n}|\leq\delta_0$ on $B(0,KN)$, $D_n\subseteq B(0,(K-1)N)$ with $K\geq a+1$, $B(0,b)\subseteq D_n$, $|b-aN|\leq C_1NQ$,
$|D_n\setminus B(0,b)|\leq C_1N^dQ$, $\delta_0\leq C_1NQ^{1/d}$ and $0\leq Q\leq1$: $|\ell_n(x)/N-2d\eps(a-|x|/N)_+|\leq CQ^{1/d}$ for all $x$.
\paragraph{Proof.} For $|x|<KN$: $\ell_n(x)=\widetilde\ell_n(x)$ and $U=U_B+U_E$ with $|U_E|\leq C\eps m^{1/d}\leq C'NQ^{1/d}$. Also
$|(b-|x|)_+-(aN-|x|)_+|\leq|b-aN|\leq C_1NQ\leq C_1NQ^{1/d}$. For $|x|\geq KN$: $x\notin A_n$, so $\ell_n(x)=0$, and $a-|x|/N<0$.
