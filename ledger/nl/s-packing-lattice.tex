% s-packing-lattice: eq:packing-lattice (backfilled twin).
\paragraph{Statement.} For any set $E$ of $k$ lattice sites and any centre $y$, $\sum_{x\in E}(1+|x-y|)^{1-d}\leq C_dk^{1/d}$.
\paragraph{Proof.} The summand decreases in $|x-y|$, so the sum is at most its value on the $k$ sites nearest $y$, which lie in a ball of radius $Ck^{1/d}$; sum over shells.
