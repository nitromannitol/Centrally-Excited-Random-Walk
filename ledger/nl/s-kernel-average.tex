\paragraph{Statement.} For $v\neq0$, $s>0$, $|v|\neq s$: $\int_{S^{d-1}}u_v\cdot N(v-s\theta)\,d\sigma(\theta)=d\omega_d|v|^{1-d}$ if $s<|v|$ and $0$ if $s>|v|$, where
$N(w)=|w|^{-d}w$ is the Newtonian field and $\sigma$ is the surface measure (\texttt{integral\_sphere\_inner\_newtonField}).
\paragraph{Proof.} Gauss flux for $N$ through the sphere of radius $s$ (\texttt{Generic/Newton/GaussFlux}) together with the swap identity; no rotation invariance is used.
