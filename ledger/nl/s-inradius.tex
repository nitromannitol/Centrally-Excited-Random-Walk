\paragraph{Statement.} For $\varepsilon>0$, $K>0$, $C_1\geq0$ there are $C>0$, $n_0$ such that for $n\geq n_0$ and a path with $X_0=0$, $b\geq0$,
$B(0,b)\subseteq D_n\subseteq\bar B(0,KN)$, $|X_n|\leq KN$, $|A_n|\leq KN^d$, $m\leq C_1N^dQ$ and $|\mathcal Q_n|\leq C_1(N\sqrt{nL}+NL)$:
$|b/N-a|\leq CQ$ with $a=((d+1)/(2d\varepsilon\omega_d))^{1/(d+1)}$.
\paragraph{Proof.} (fl:1013--1058.) Apply \texttt{exists\_abs\_div\_sub\_le} with $C_1'$ large, $S=\sum_{A_n}|x|$, $I=\int_{D_n}|v|$, $I_E=\int_{E}|v|$,
$\mathcal Q_n=$ the quadratic Dynkin martingale and $X_{\rm sq}=|X_n|^2$. Its hypotheses: \texttt{sq\_euclidNorm\_eq} (the identity);
$0\leq X_{\rm sq}\leq K^2N^2$; \texttt{abs\_sum\_euclidNorm\_sub\_integral\_le} and $|A_n|\leq KN^d$ ($|S-I|\leq C N^d$);
\texttt{integral\_norm\_eq\_ball\_add} with $S=KN$ ($I_E\leq KNm\leq KC_1nQ$, as $N^{d+1}=n$); $|\mathcal Q_n|\leq C nQ$ and $N^{1-d}\leq Q$, $N^{-1}\leq Q$ for large $n$
(for $d=2$, $N\sqrt{nL}=nQ$ exactly; for $d\geq3$, $N\sqrt{nL}/(nQ)\to0$; $NL/(nQ)\to0$).
