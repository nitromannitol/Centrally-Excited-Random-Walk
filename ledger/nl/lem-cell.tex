\paragraph{Paper (verbatim).}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:cell}
There exists $C(d)<\infty$ such that, for every $x\in\Z^d$,
\begin{equation*}
\int_{C_x}|\nabla \gauge(v)-\xi(x)|\dd v\leq C\mu\bigl(B(x,6\sqrt d)\bigr)\, .
\end{equation*}
\end{lemma}
Definitions the statement relies on (verbatim):
\textit{$\mu$ and eq:hessian-growth (lines 458--461)}
Let $\mu$ be the distributional Laplacian of~$\gauge$ on~$\R^d$, so that $\int\phi\dd\mu=\int \gauge(v)\sum_{i=1}^d\partial_i^2\phi(v)\dd v$ for every smooth compactly supported function~$\phi$ on~$\R^d$. The distributional Laplacian~$\mu$ is a positive Radon measure, because the smooth convex functions obtained by mollifying~$\gauge$ have nonnegative Laplacian. For $y\in\R^d$ and $R>0$, let $\phi$ be smooth with $0\leq\phi\leq1$, $\phi=1$ on~$B(y,R)$, support in~$B(y,2R)$ and $|\nabla\phi|\leq2/R$. Integrating by parts and using $|\nabla \gauge|\leq\Lambda_\gauge$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hessian-growth}
\mu(B(y,R))\leq\int\phi\dd\mu=-\int\nabla \gauge\cdot\nabla\phi\leq2^{d+1}\omega_d\Lambda_\gauge R^{d-1}\, .
\end{equation}

\emph{Reading choices.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$\mu$ is quantified.} The draft states the lemma for every locally finite measure satisfying the defining identity, rather than for a named $\mu$. Two locally finite measures with the same integrals against all $C_c^\infty$ functions coincide, so the hypothesis pins the paper's $\mu$; the paper shows that it exists (positive Radon measure, by mollification, lines 458--459). The statement is therefore equivalent to the paper's, \emph{provided} that such a measure exists for every norm. The existence lemma is not yet in the repository; it is required for non-vacuity and is used by the proof of lem-local (via eq:hessian-growth).
\item \textbf{Quantifier order.} \verb!∃ C, 0 < C ∧ ∀ Ψ, IsNorm Ψ → ∀ ξ, .. → ∀ m, .. → ∀ x, ..!: $C=C(d)$ is chosen before $\gauge$, before $\xi$, before $\mu$ and before $x$, as the paper states $C(d)$. (The paper's proof indeed gives a constant depending on $d$ only: the statement is homogeneous in $\gauge$, $h(x)=0$, and the mollification bound is dimensional.)
\item The inequality is in \verb!ℝ≥0∞!: \verb!ENNReal.ofReal (∫ ..) ≤ ENNReal.ofReal C * m (ball ..)!. The integral is nonnegative, so \verb!ofReal! is exact; $m$ is finite on balls (locally finite), so the right side is finite.
\item The constant $C$ is bound with \verb!0 < C! (the paper's $C<\infty$, nonnegativity being without loss of generality).
\end{enumerate}
