% s-outer-of-mass: the outer inclusion on the event, given mass, envelope and inradius.
\paragraph{Statement.} For $\varepsilon,C_0,C_1>0$, $C_m\geq0$, $b_d\geq1$ there are $C>0$, $n_0$: for $n\geq n_0$, on $\mathrm{fluctEvent}$ with $b$ the inradius,

$C_1$-slot and $C_2=C_m$: $H_n\leq b+C'WL$. \texttt{exists\_outer\_inclusion} with $C_2=\max(C_m,C')$.
