% s-occupation: pathwise facts about local times and ranges (backfilled twin).
\paragraph{Statement.} $\sum_{x\in A_n}\ell_n(x)=n$, hence $n\leq M_nR_n$; $k_{0,n}=R_n$; $\ell_{s,t}\leq\ell_t$; a nearest-neighbour path from $0$ has $|X_j|\leq j$; $A_n$ lies in the lattice ball of radius $H_n$.
\paragraph{Proof.} Counting: each time $j<n$ contributes to exactly one local time; each site of $A_n$ has one first departure; induction on $j$ with the triangle inequality.
