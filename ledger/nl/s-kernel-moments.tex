% s-kernel-moments: the algebra of the first-departure kernel (backfilled twin).
\paragraph{Statement.} For $0\leq\varepsilon<1/d$ the first-departure probabilities $q_x(e)$ and the SRW step probabilities are supported on the $2d$ unit steps,
are nonnegative and sum to one; $\sum_eq_x(e)e=-\varepsilon u_x$ and the SRW mean is $0$; the same for \texttt{stepProb} in either case.
\paragraph{Proof.} (fl:315--340, eq:kernel.) Finite sums over the $2d$ unit vectors: $q_x(\pm e_i)=\frac1{2d}(1\mp d\varepsilon\,u_x\cdot e_i)$, nonnegative since $|u_x\cdot e_i|\leq1$ and $d\varepsilon<1$.
