\paragraph{Statement.} (i) $P|\cdot|^2(x)=|x|^2+1$ for $d\geq1$. (ii) $D|\cdot|^2(x)=2x$. (iii) For a path from the origin,
$|X_n|^2=n-2\eps\sum_{x\in A_n}|x|+\mathcal Q_n$, with $\mathcal Q$ the Dynkin martingale of $|\cdot|^2$.
\paragraph{Proof.} (i) $\frac1{2d}\sum_i(|x+e_i|^2+|x-e_i|^2)=\frac1{2d}\sum_i(2|x|^2+2)$. (ii) $\frac12(|x+e_i|^2-|x-e_i|^2)=2x_i$. (iii) By
s-step-mean and (i), (ii), the compensator increment is $1-\eps I_ju_{X_j}\cdot2X_j=1-2\eps I_j|X_j|$. The first departures from nonzero sites
sum over $A_n\setminus\{0\}$ (s-fresh-sum), and $|0|=0$.
