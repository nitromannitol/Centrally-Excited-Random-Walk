\paragraph{Paper (verbatim).} ``Let $d\geq2$ be an integer, let $\eps>0$, and let $D\subset\R^d$ be
a bounded measurable set of volume $R$. Let $U_D(y)=(2\eps/\omega_d)\int_Du_v\cdot(v-y)|v-y|^{-d}\dd v$,
and let $F(s)=\sigma_d^{-1}\int_{D\cap\{|v|>s\}}|v|^{1-d}\dd v$. Then there is a constant $C_d>0$,
depending only on $d$, such that for every $y,z\in\R^d$ and every $s>0$,
$\|U_D\|_\infty\leq C_d\eps R^{1/d}$, $|U_D(y)-U_D(z)|\leq C_d\eps R^{1/(2d)}|y-z|^{1/2}$,
$\frac1{\sigma_d}\int_{S^{d-1}}U_D(s\theta)\dd S(\theta)=2d\eps F(s)$. For every $b>0$, the ball
potential is $U_{B(0,b)}(y)=2d\eps(b-|y|)_+$.''
\paragraph{Proof.} Write $K(w)=w/|w|^d$, so $U_D(y)=\frac{2\eps}{\omega_d}\int_D u_v\cdot K(v-y)\,dv$
and $|u_v\cdot K(v-y)|\le|v-y|^{1-d}$.
(1) \emph{Sup bound.} By \texttt{s-potential-integrable}, $\int_D|v-y|^{1-d}dv\le\int_{B(y,\rho)}|v-y|^{1-d}dv=\sigma_d\rho$
with $\omega_d\rho^d=R$. The inequality is the bathtub principle
(\texttt{Generic.Kernel.setIntegral\_le\_ball\_of\_antitoneOn}); the equality is polar coordinates.
Hence $|U_D(y)|\le\frac{2\eps}{\omega_d}\sigma_d(R/\omega_d)^{1/d}=C_d\eps R^{1/d}$
(\texttt{s-potential-bound}).
(2) \emph{H\"older.} With $q=2d/(2d-1)$, \texttt{s-kernel-modulus} gives
$\|K(\cdot-y)-K(\cdot-z)\|_{L^q}=c_d|y-z|^{1/2}$. H\"older's inequality with exponents $2d$ and $q$
against $\ind_Du$ (whose $L^{2d}$ norm is at most $R^{1/(2d)}$) gives the bound (\texttt{s-holder}).
(3) \emph{Spherical mean.} By \texttt{s-kernel-average}, for $|v|\ne s$,
$\sigma_d^{-1}\int_S u_v\cdot K(v-s\theta)\,dS(\theta)=|v|^{1-d}\ind_{\{|v|>s\}}$. This follows from
Gauss's law (\texttt{s-gauss-flux}): the integrand, as a function of $\theta$, is the flux integrand
of the unit source at $s\hat v$ through the sphere of radius $|v|$. The sphere $\{|v|=s\}$ is null.
Fubini applies because $\sup_y\int_D|v-y|^{1-d}<\infty$ (\texttt{s-potential-integrable}). Integrating
over $D$ gives $2d\eps F(s)$, since $\sigma_d/\omega_d=d$ (\texttt{s-newton}).
(4) \emph{Ball.} In polar coordinates $v=t\theta$ with $t<b$,
$U_{B(0,b)}(y)=\frac{2\eps}{\omega_d}\int_0^b t^{d-1}\int_S\theta\cdot K(t\theta-y)\,dS\,dt$. By
\texttt{s-gauss-flux} the inner integral is $\sigma_dt^{1-d}\ind_{\{|y|<t\}}$ for $t\ne|y|$, so
$U_{B(0,b)}(y)=2d\eps\int_0^b\ind_{\{|y|<t\}}dt=2d\eps(b-|y|)_+$ (\texttt{s-ballpotential}).
Every constant above depends only on $d$, so $C_d$ is chosen before $\eps$.
\paragraph{Transcription notes.} $\|U_D\|_\infty$ is written $\forall y$ (R4). The surface measure
is \texttt{volume.toSphere} with total mass $d\omega_d=\sigma_d$. $R=(\mathrm{vol}\,D).\mathrm{toReal}$
is honest because $D$ is bounded. The Bochner integrals are integrable for bounded measurable $D$
(declared hazard). $C_d$ is bound before $\eps$ (R-15). The ball clause shares the same $\eps$,
as in the paper.
