% s-volume: eq:volume and the symmetric difference (deterministic).
\paragraph{Statement.} If $B(0,b)\subseteq D$, $|D\setminus B(0,b)|\leq C_1N^dQ$, $|b/N-a|\leq C_1Q$ and $0\leq Q\leq1$, then $|(N^{-1}D)\Delta B(0,a)|+||D|/N^d-\omega_da^d|\leq CQ$.
\paragraph{Proof.} $N^{-1}D=B(0,b/N)\cup N^{-1}E$, so the symmetric difference lies in $(B(0,b/N)\Delta B(0,a))\cup N^{-1}E$. Its volume is at most
$\omega_d|(b/N)^d-a^d|+N^{-d}|E|\leq\omega_dd(a+C_1)^{d-1}C_1Q+C_1Q$. Likewise $|D|/N^d-\omega_da^d=\omega_d((b/N)^d-a^d)+N^{-d}|E|$.
